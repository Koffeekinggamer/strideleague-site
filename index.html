How a race goes

  1. 1

    Make a race

    Pick a short race and a handful of friends. Up to 5 people free, or up to 100 with Stride League Plus.

  2. 2

    Send the link

    If they have Stride League, the link opens the app. If they don’t, it sends them to get it.

  3. 3

    Walk

    Pick one step source, and your steps update the race.

Questions

How many people can be in a race?

Up to 5 people on the free tier, or up to 100 with Stride League Plus.

Which phones and watches work?

On iPhone, pick Apple Watch, iPhone, or both, through Apple Health. On Android, pick this phone or a Samsung Galaxy Watch, through Health Connect. Each race counts steps from your one source.

How to choose your step source

What happens when a race ends?

When a race ends, standings stay open for 24 hours so late syncs from your watch or phone can still count. Steps must still fall inside the race dates. After that, the winner and podium lock.

Is there a widget?

Home-screen widget (Android). See steps today and where you stand in an active race without opening the app. Tap to open that race in Stride League. iPhone widget coming after App Store setup for the extension.

Your steps stay in the race

We read steps to show the race. We don’t sell them, and we don’t use them for ads.
