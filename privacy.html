Privacy

Stride League is a race on steps for a few friends. This page says what we collect, why we collect it, and who else handles it.

Your account

You can sign in with your email, with Apple, or with Google. We store the email address on your account and the sign-in identifier Apple or Google gives us. We never receive your Apple or Google password.

Steps

On iPhone, Stride League reads steps from Apple Health. On Android, it reads steps from Health Connect. We use those steps only to show your own totals and race standings.

The only health data we read is your daily step total, from the one step source you choose. On iPhone, that’s iPhone, Apple Watch, or both together. On Android, it’s this phone, or Samsung Health for a Samsung Galaxy Watch. You have one step source at a time. Only steps recorded by the source you choose count.

To tell sources apart, the app also reads which app or device recorded your steps. It may look at recent step records on your phone to do this, but those records stay on your phone.

With each daily step total, we upload:

  • The source label, which names the app or device that recorded the steps. On Android, this includes the recording app’s package name.
  • Your phone’s model name. This is the phone, not your watch.
  • The time of the sync.

Your source label is shared with the race and shows on the standings. Your phone model is visible only to you. Other people in your races can’t see it. We use it only for fair-play checks.

Stride League doesn’t connect to your accounts with other fitness companies. It reads only what you allow through Apple Health or Health Connect. We don’t read heart rate, sleep, location, workouts, or any other health data, and we never write anything to Apple Health or Health Connect.

We don’t sell your information. We don’t use steps or other Health data for advertising.

We don’t share Health data with third parties beyond our backend (Supabase) and subscription processing (Apple, Google, and RevenueCat).

Turning off Health access

You can stop Stride League from reading steps at any time. The app still opens, and your totals stay empty instead of showing zero.

  • On iPhone, open the Health app, tap your picture, tap Apps, tap Stride League, and turn off Steps.
  • On Android, open Health Connect, tap App permissions, tap Stride League, and turn off Steps. On Android 14 and later, Health Connect is in Settings.

To turn access back on, follow the same steps and turn Steps on.

Fair play

If a day has more than 60,000 steps, the person who created that race sees a fair-play flag for that day. Other people in the race don’t see it. A high day isn’t an automatic ban or removal from a race. A single sync of more than 200,000 steps for one day is rejected.

Races

A race holds up to 5 people on the free tier, or up to 100 with Stride League Plus. When a race ends, there is a 24-hour window for late step syncs. Only steps inside the race dates count. After that window, the result locks on our servers.

Home-screen widget (Android)

On Android, the home-screen widget shows your steps today and your standing in an active race. To do that, the app keeps a small snapshot in Android shared preferences on your phone. The snapshot is not uploaded for the widget.

Signing out or deleting your account clears the snapshot. Removing the widget from your home screen does not clear it.

Reports and blocks

You can report or block another person in the app. We store the report so we can review it. A block keeps that person out of your races.

Stride League Plus

Plus is $3.99 a month and renews automatically until you cancel. Cancel in your Apple ID settings. Apple, Google, and RevenueCat process the subscription. We learn whether Plus is active. We don’t receive your full payment card number.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app. We then delete the account data we store, including your profile and your personal day-by-day step totals. You come off races that haven’t locked yet, including a race still in its 24-hour window. On a race that has already locked, the step totals already counted stay so the result doesn’t change, your name is removed, and the board shows Former member.
