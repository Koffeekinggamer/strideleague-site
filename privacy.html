Privacy

Stride League is a race on steps for a few friends. This page says what we collect, why we collect it, and who else handles it.

Your account

You can sign in with your email, with Apple, or with Google. We store the email address on your account and the sign-in identifier Apple or Google gives us. We never receive your Apple or Google password.

Steps

On iPhone, Stride League reads steps from Apple Health. On Android, it reads steps from Health Connect. We use those steps only to show race standings.

We don’t sell your information. We don’t use steps or other Health data for advertising.

We don’t share Health data with third parties beyond our backend (Supabase) and subscription processing (Apple, Google, and RevenueCat).

Races

A race holds up to 5 people on the free tier, or up to 100 with Stride League Plus. When a race ends, there is a 24-hour window for late step syncs. Only steps inside the race dates count. After that window, the result locks on our servers.

Home-screen widget (Android)

On Android, the home-screen widget shows your steps today and your standing in an active race. To do that, the app keeps a small snapshot in Android shared preferences on your phone. The snapshot is not uploaded for the widget.

Signing out or deleting your account clears the snapshot. Removing the widget from your home screen does not clear it.

Reports and blocks

You can report or block another person in the app. We store the report so we can review it. A block keeps that person out of your races.

Stride League Plus

Plus is $3.99 a month and renews automatically until you cancel. Cancel in your Apple ID settings. Apple, Google, and RevenueCat process the subscription. We learn whether Plus is active. We don’t receive your full payment card number.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app. We then delete the account data we store, including your profile and your personal day-by-day step totals. You come off races that haven’t locked yet, including a race still in its 24-hour window. On a race that has already locked, the step totals already counted stay so the result doesn’t change, your name is removed, and the board shows Former member.

This website

strideleague.me doesn’t use tracking cookies or third-party analytics.
