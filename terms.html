Terms

These terms cover the Stride League app and strideleague.me. By using them, you agree to this page.

The app

Stride League lets a small group race on step counts. Standings are there so you can see how the race is going. They aren’t medical advice.

Your account

You can sign in with email, Apple, or Google. You’re responsible for what happens on your account. You can delete the account in the app. Deletion removes the account data we store, including your profile and the race standings tied to you.

Racing with other people

Keep it kind. Don’t harass people, misuse an invite, or fake steps. You can report or block another person in the app. We may remove someone from a race, or close an account, when that’s needed to keep Stride League safe.

Health data

On iPhone, step counts are read from Apple Health. On Android, they’re read from Health Connect. We use them only to show race standings. We don’t sell them, and we don’t use them for ads.

We don’t share Health data with third parties beyond our backend (Supabase) and subscription processing (Apple, Google, and RevenueCat).

Stride League Plus

Plus is $3.99 a month. The subscription renews automatically until you cancel. Cancel in your Apple ID settings. Canceling stops the next renewal. Apple handles payment. When the Play Store listing is available, Google handles payment there.

We don’t receive your full payment card number. RevenueCat helps us know whether Plus is active.

Links

Sign-in, invite, and share links are meant to open Stride League when the app is installed. If it isn’t, the link opens this website so you can get the app.
