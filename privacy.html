Privacy

Stride League is a step-count race for a few friends. This page says what we collect, why we collect it, and who else handles it.

Your account

You can sign in with your email, with Apple, or with Google. We store the email address on your account and the sign-in identifier Apple or Google gives us. We never receive your Apple or Google password.

Step counts

On iPhone, Stride League reads step counts from Apple Health. On Android, it reads step counts from Health Connect. We use those counts only to show race standings.

We don’t sell your information. We don’t use step counts, or other Health data, for advertising.

We don’t share Health data with third parties beyond our backend (Supabase) and subscription processing (Apple, Google, and RevenueCat).

Reports and blocks

You can report or block another person in the app. We keep the report so we can review it, and we keep the block so that person stays out of your races.

Stride League Plus

Plus is $3.99 a month and renews automatically until you cancel. Cancel in your Apple ID settings. Apple, Google, and RevenueCat process the subscription. We learn whether Plus is active. We don’t receive your full payment card number.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app. We then delete the account data we store, including your profile and the race standings tied to you.

This website

strideleague.me doesn’t use tracking cookies or third-party analytics.
