Terms

These terms cover the Stride League app and strideleague.me. By using the app or this site, you agree to these terms.

The app

Stride League lets a small group race on steps. A race holds up to 5 people on the free tier, or up to 100 with Stride League Plus. When the race ends, there is a 24-hour window for late step syncs, then the result locks. Only steps inside the race dates count. Standings show how the race is going. They aren’t medical advice.

Your account

You can sign in with email, Apple, or Google. You’re responsible for what happens on your account. You can delete the account in the app. Deletion removes the account data we store, including your profile and your personal step totals. You come off races that haven’t locked yet. On a race that has already locked, the counted step totals stay so the result doesn’t change, and your name is replaced with Former member. The privacy policy has the details.

Racing with other people

Keep it kind. Don’t harass people, misuse an invite, or fake steps. You can report or block another person in the app. We may remove someone from a race, or close an account, when that’s needed to keep Stride League safe.

Fair play

Each race counts steps from one source you choose, such as iPhone, Apple Watch, this phone, or Samsung Health. Steps from other apps don’t count unless that app is your source. Steps you type in by hand don’t count.

You can change your step source in the app once every 24 hours. A change applies to races you join afterward. In a race you’re already in, you can switch once. The new source starts the next day, and the standings show that you changed source.

If a day has more than 60,000 steps, the person who created that race sees a fair-play flag for that day. Other people in the race don’t see it. A day over 200,000 steps isn’t accepted. Flags never ban anyone automatically. A report is still the way to raise a concern, and we review reports within 24 hours.

Health data

On iPhone, steps are read from Apple Health. On Android, they’re read from Health Connect. We read the daily step total from the one source you choose, and which app or device recorded it. We use steps only to show your own totals and race standings. We don’t sell them, and we don’t use them for ads.

We don’t share Health data with third parties beyond our backend (Supabase) and subscription processing (Apple, Google, and RevenueCat).

Stride League Plus

Plus is $3.99 a month and renews automatically until you cancel. Cancel in your Apple ID settings. Canceling stops the next renewal. Apple handles payment. When the Play Store listing is available, Google handles payment there.

We don’t receive your full payment card number. RevenueCat helps us know whether Plus is active.

Links

Sign-in, invite, and share links are meant to open Stride League when the app is installed. If it isn’t, the link opens this website so you can get the app.
