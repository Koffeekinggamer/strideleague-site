The app

Stride League lets a small group race on steps. A race holds up to 5 people, or up to 20 if it was created with Stride League Plus. It runs for a limited time (about 31 days or less; the presets are 7, 14, and 30 days), and uses one timezone. When the time is up, there is a 24-hour window for late step syncs, then the result locks. Only steps inside the race dates count. Standings show how the race is going. They aren’t medical advice. Ties share the place. A rematch is a new race, not a reopening of the locked one.

Your account

You can delete the account in the app. Deletion removes the account data we store, including your profile and your personal step totals. You come off races that haven’t locked yet. On a race that has already locked, the counted step totals stay so the result doesn’t change, and your name is replaced with Former member. The privacy policy has the details.

Friend connections, one-to-one messages, blocks, and the record that you accepted the terms are deleted with the account. Reports you filed, and reports that were filed about you, are kept with your identity removed, for safety review. Messages you’ve already sent in a group or race chat stay visible to its members.

Fair play

Each race counts steps from one source you choose, such as iPhone, Apple Watch, this phone, or Samsung Health. Only steps recorded by the source you choose count.

You can change your step source in the app once every 24 hours. A change applies to races you join afterward. In a race you’re already in, you can switch once. The new source starts the next day, and the standings show that you changed source.

If a day has more than 60,000 steps, the person who created that race sees a fair-play flag for that day. Other people in the race don’t see it. A single sync of more than 200,000 steps for one day is rejected. A high day isn’t an automatic ban or removal from a race. A report is still the way to raise a concern, and we review reports within 24 hours.

Health data

On iPhone, steps are read from Apple Health. On Android, they’re read from Health Connect. We read the daily step total from the one source you choose, and which app or device recorded it. We use steps only to show your own totals and race standings. We don’t sell them, and we don’t use them for ads.

Step data goes only to our backend (Supabase). We don’t share it with other companies.

Joining a race is free. The app has no advertising. An account does not expire. Stride League Plus is the optional subscription in the next section.

Stride League Plus

Stride League Plus is an auto-renewing subscription sold by Judson Miller on iPhone, in the United States, through Apple In-App Purchase. The length is one month. The price is $3.99 per month.

Plus lets you create a race of up to 20 people. Without Plus, a race you create holds up to 5 people. Joining a race is free either way.

Apple charges the payment to your Apple ID when you confirm the purchase. Plus renews each month unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current month ends. If you do not cancel in time, Apple charges your Apple ID $3.99 for the next month within the 24 hours before that month ends.

To manage or cancel Plus, open Settings on the iPhone, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, and tap Stride League Plus. Canceling stops the next renewal. It does not refund the month already paid. Refunds go through Apple. Judson Miller does not send the refund.

Deleting your Stride League account does not cancel Plus. Cancel in iPhone Settings, or Apple keeps renewing the charge.

If Plus ends, a race you already created keeps the higher limit it already had. It is not cut down to 5 people, and the people already in it stay. People can still join that race until it reaches that limit. A new race you create after Plus ends holds up to 5 people.

RevenueCat tells the app whether Plus is active. It receives the app user ID and the purchase status. It does not receive payment card data. The Privacy Policy says the same thing.
