Delete your Stride League account

You can ask for your Stride League account and the personal data that goes with it to be deleted here. You do not need the app installed, and you do not need to download it again.

This page is the web deletion link for the app: https://strideleague.me/delete-account/

Delete in the app

  1. Open Stride League.
  2. Go to the You tab.
  3. Choose Delete account.
  4. Confirm.

Delete by email

If you cannot open the app, email nckoffeeking@gmail.com from the email you can check.

Use the subject line Delete my Stride League account and include:

  • Your display name in the app
  • Whether you signed in with email, with Apple, or with Google
  • The email address on that account, if you know it
  • A sentence that you want the Stride League account deleted

What is deleted

You can delete your account in the app. We then delete the account data we store, including your profile, your push token, and your personal day-by-day step totals. You come off races that haven’t locked yet, including a race still in its 24-hour window.

Deleting your account doesn’t cancel your Plus subscription.

To cancel, open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

What is kept

On a race that has already locked, the step totals already counted stay so the result doesn’t change, your name is removed, and the board shows Former member.

The same rules are in the Privacy Policy.
